## Assumptions (single-shot, stated up front)

1. **"KRAS G12C in PDAC" is rare** — G12C is only ~1–2% of PDAC KRAS mutations (G12D ~40%, G12V ~29%, G12R ~15% dominate) ([Weill Cornell](https://news.weill.cornell.edu/news/2024/10/researchers-develop-insights-into-kras-mutations-in-pancreatic-cancers), [Clin Cancer Res 2025](https://aacrjournals.org/clincancerres/article/31/6/1082/753262/Distinct-Molecular-and-Clinical-Features-of)). Because G12C-specific human PDAC data exists for only two drugs, I've broadened the screen to **KRAS-pathway members** (MAPK, PI3K/AKT/mTOR, EGFR upstream, autophagy, prenylation, SRC, JAK/STAT3, Hedgehog) as you explicitly allowed, since nearly all PDAC (~90–95%) is KRAS-mutant regardless of allele ([JCI 2025](https://www.jci.org/articles/view/191939), [PMC11055996](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11055996/)).
2. **Disease context** = locally advanced/metastatic PDAC, since that's where essentially all repurposing trials have enrolled.
3. **"Approved"** = FDA-approved for at least one indication (most also EMA-approved); I flag the one exception (lonafarnib class) explicitly.
4. Target validation: pancreatic cancer is the single most KRAS-dependent lineage in genome-wide CRISPR dependency screens (DepMap), ahead of stomach/colon/lung ([PMC8493485](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8493485/)), which underwrites pathway-level repurposing logic even where G12C-specific data is absent.

## How to read this for triage

- **Only rows 1–2** have direct human evidence in the actual G12C-mutant PDAC population. Everything else is pan-KRAS/pathway rationale in unselected or non-G12C-selected PDAC — useful for combination hypotheses with sotorasib/adagrasib, not standalone G12C activity.
- **Best combination logic given CYP liabilities**: sotorasib/adagrasib (rows 1–2) already carry CYP3A4 substrate/inducer/inhibitor complexity, which rules out itraconazole (14), simvastatin (9), and everolimus (12) as *simultaneous* triplet partners without exposure modeling — sequence or dose-adjust rather than co-administer.
- **Negative phase 2/3 readouts on pathway monotherapy** (trametinib, selumetinib, dasatinib, ruxolitinib/JANUS, metformin) argue against single-agent repurposing and for pairing with a G12C inhibitor specifically to block resistance/bypass signaling — which is exactly the panitumumab (row 3) logic, still untested in PDAC.
- **Most tractable near-term repurposing bets**: disulfiram (active trial, clean mechanistic rationale, cheap) and nelfinavir (clean phase 1 signal, defined AKT mechanism) — both worth a focused KRAS G12C + these agents preclinical combo screen before committing trial resources.

**Caveat on niclosamide/ivermectin-class evidence**: that PDAC clinical claim traces to a secondary blog aggregator, not a primary trial registration I could independently verify — flagging it so it doesn't get over-weighted relative to the primary-source rows.
