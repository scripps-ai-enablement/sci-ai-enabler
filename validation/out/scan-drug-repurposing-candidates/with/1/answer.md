## Assumptions made (single-shot, no follow-up)
- **"KRAS G12C in PDAC"** is interpreted broadly: (a) the ~1–2% of PDAC tumors that are genuinely G12C-mutant, where direct G12C inhibitors apply, plus (b) approved drugs against KRAS pathway nodes (RTK, RAF/MEK/ERK, PI3K/AKT/mTOR, CDK4/6, autophagy, prenylation) that are mechanistically and clinically relevant as combination/repurposing partners in KRAS-mutant PDAC generally, since restricting strictly to G12C alone leaves too small an evidence base for a repurposing shortlist.
- "Approved" = FDA-approved for *some* indication (not necessarily PDAC or KRAS G12C).
- "Clinical-stage" = either already in human PDAC trials, or an FDA-approved oncology drug being proposed for a *new* PDAC combination based on preclinical/mechanistic rationale.

## Key caveats for the screen design
1. **G12C is genuinely rare in PDAC (~1–2% of KRAS-mutant cases, ~500–1,000 US cases/yr)** — Tier 1 agents require NGS-based patient selection; broad-population testing will mostly recruit for the Tier 2/3 pathway-node rationale rather than G12C-specific mechanism.
2. **CYP3A4 traffic-jam risk**: adagrasib, everolimus/sirolimus/temsirolimus, ribociclib, selumetinib, simvastatin, palbociclib, and cobimetinib are all strong CYP3A4 substrates or perpetrators — combining any two of these (e.g., adagrasib + a statin or an mTOR inhibitor) needs a formal DDI/PK bridging study before dosing.
3. **Negative precedent exists and should temper expectations**: everolimus, simvastatin, and metformin all failed to show clinical benefit in prospective PDAC trials despite sound mechanistic rationale — don't over-weight preclinical genetic rationale alone.
4. **Best-supported near-term bets**: sotorasib/adagrasib (biomarker-matched, positive basket-trial data), the defactinib+avutometinib RAMP 205 combination (83% ORR signal, orphan designation), and binimetinib (cleanest DDI profile among MEK inhibitors for combination building).
5. Live numeric Open Targets association scores and DepMap Chronos dependency values could not be pulled programmatically in this session — confirm directly at platform.opentargets.org and depmap.org before finalizing prioritization weights.
