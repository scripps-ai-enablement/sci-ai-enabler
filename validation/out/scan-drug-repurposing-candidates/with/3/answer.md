## Critical caveat before the table

**KRAS G12C is a minority allele in PDAC.** Unlike NSCLC (where G12C dominates), PDAC's KRAS mutations are ~90% G12D/G12V/G12R, and G12C shows up in only **~1.7–2%** of cases (AACR GENIE cohort, n=3,755, *Clin Cancer Res* 2025: G12C 2%; Foundation Medicine real-world data: G12C 1.7%, *JCO* 2025 abstract). That means: only sotorasib and adagrasib are *direct on-target* G12C plays, and they require **biomarker-selected patients**. Everything else below is repurposing logic against the broader **KRAS/MAPK pathway**, testable either in the rare G12C-PDAC subset or as pathway-node comparators in KRAS-mutant PDAC generally — I've flagged which applies per row. I could not retrieve a numeric Open Targets association score directly (site is JS-rendered, GraphQL call 500'd) — DepMap CRISPR data does independently confirm KRAS as a core dependency in KRAS-mutant PDAC lines (*Science* 2024, *Nat Commun* 2017).

## Notes and gaps (say-so per the grounding rule)

- **SHP2 (PTPN11) and SOS1 inhibitors are excluded** — RMC-4630, TNO155, JAB-3312 (SHP2) and BI-1701963 (SOS1) are all investigational, none FDA-approved, so they don't fit an "approved compound" repurposing screen; SHERPA (NCT04916236) is the trial to watch if that changes.
- ChEMBL/DGIdb numeric bioactivity panels (pChEMBL values) weren't retrievable in this session beyond on-label pharmacology — liability data above is FDA label/DailyMed-sourced, not a systematic polypharmacology screen. If you want quantitative off-target hit panels, the `profile-compound-polypharmacology` recipe (ChEMBL connector) is the next step per compound.
- Open Targets' numeric KRAS–pancreatic-carcinoma association score couldn't be pulled programmatically (JS-rendered site, GraphQL 500 error) — worth a manual check at `platform.opentargets.org/target/ENSG00000133703/associations` before finalizing weighting.
