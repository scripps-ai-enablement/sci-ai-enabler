## Assumptions made (single-shot, no follow-up)
- **Screen format**: since KRAS G12C is only ~1–2% of KRAS-mutant PDAC (vs. G12D ~40–47%, G12V ~28–34%; Bekaii-Saab et al., *Clin Cancer Res* 2025), I'm treating this as a **combination/resistance-overcoming screen** (approved drugs tested alongside or against a G12C background/positive-control line such as MIA PaCa-2), not a monotherapy screen — that's consistent with how the field has actually approached this rare allele.
- "Approved" = FDA-approved for *any* indication (repurposing), including the two direct G12C inhibitors, which serve as positive controls rather than novel repurposing hits.
- Where a drug has no PDAC-specific trial but strong pathway/mechanistic rationale and trial data in another KRAS-mutant tumor, I include it but flag the evidence gap explicitly rather than implying PDAC-specific proof.

## Why these targets: genetic/expression rationale (applies pathway-wide)
- KRAS mutated in ~90–92% of PDAC (TCGA-PAAD; ASCO 2025 real-world cohort JCO.2025.43.4_suppl.777); DepMap/Achilles CRISPR shows KRAS is a common-essential dependency in PDAC lines, and MIA PaCa-2 (G12C) shows selective, non-redundant KRAS dependency (PLOS Comput Biol 2023).
- CPTAC proteogenomics (Cao et al., *Cell* 2021) shows elevated MAPK/ERK and PI3K-AKT-mTOR pathway activity in PDAC tumor vs. adjacent-normal tissue; cross-validated against the ERK-dependent phosphoproteome (Papke/Der et al., *Science* 2024).
- PDAC has a well-documented **KRAS-driven autophagy addiction** (Guo et al., *Genes Dev* 2011; Yang et al., *Genes Dev* 2011/2014), and synthetic-lethal/resistance nodes include SHOC2–MEK (Wong et al., *Cell Rep* 2019) and YAP1/TEAD (CRISPR screens, *Cancer Res* 2024–25), which is why MEK-, EGFR-, and autophagy-directed repurposing candidates dominate the shortlist below rather than direct KRAS binders.

## Reconciliation notes for screen design
- **Don't screen for direct G12C-selective monotherapy hits among repurposed drugs** — none of the 12 non-KRASi candidates act on KRAS directly; all work through prenylation, autophagy, or parallel/downstream effector nodes. Use sotorasib/adagrasib strictly as positive controls.
- **The single best-supported mechanistic combination axis** is autophagy inhibition (HCQ) + MEK inhibition (trametinib/binimetinib/selumetinib) ± EGFR blockade (erlotinib), which directly targets the two resistance mechanisms PDAC uses against pathway blockade (feedback RTK reactivation and autophagy-fueled metabolic rescue).
- **Deprioritize** simvastatin, metformin, and everolimus monotherapy arms — each has a negative or neutral randomized/phase II PDAC trial already; they're only worth re-testing as part of a combination (e.g., atorvastatin instead of simvastatin for a cleaner CYP profile; sirolimus+metformin as a paired arm).
- **Trial-feasibility red flags** to weight into any go/no-go: adagrasib (QTc + broad CYP3A4/2D6), lonafarnib and itraconazole-class agents (bidirectional CYP3A entanglement), hydroxychloroquine (irreversible retinal toxicity limiting chronic dosing duration), and nelfinavir (potent CYP3A4/2C19 inhibition complicating co-dosing with most oncology TKIs).
- **Evidence gap flagged explicitly, not glossed over**: I could not find a published DepMap comparison isolating "G12C PDAC lines" dependency magnitude vs. other KRAS-allele PDAC lines beyond single-line (MIA PaCa-2) data — worth generating internally as your screen's own baseline before ranking hits.
