# Curator state

## Recently surfaced

- **AtomisticSkills** (added 2026-05-31) — MIT (Gómez-Bombarelli and Coley groups) with Shell open-source agent harness empowering general-purpose AI coding agents to conduct atomistic research across materials, chemistry, and drug discovery via 100+ curated MCP tools and skills; validated on six campaigns (Li-ion solid-state electrolyte design, MOF CO2 capture screening, autonomous MLIP benchmarking and fine-tuning, structure-based virtual screening, multimodal XRD analysis, Fe-oxide OER catalyst screening).
- **ScientistOne** (added 2026-05-31) — Google Cloud AI Research end-to-end autonomous research system designed around the Chain-of-Evidence (CoE) standard; achieves 0/337 hallucinated references, 12/12 score verification, 14/15 method-code alignment across 75 audited papers from five systems on five ADRS frontier ML tasks; generalizes to medical imaging, fine-grained recognition, 3D perception, parameter-constrained LM with state-of-the-art on Parameter Golf and gold medals on MLE-Bench.
- **NORA** (added 2026-05-31) — University of Tennessee, Knoxville / Emory harness-engineered multi-agent autonomous research system purpose-built for GIScience and spatial data science; 21 domain-specialized workflow skills, 9 specialist sub-agents, custom MCP servers; introduces two novel skill units (spatial analysis decision frameworks; reproducible geospatial data download); evaluated by 6 domain specialists + 3 LLM reviewers on 7 dimensions.
- **MCI** (added 2026-05-31) — KRICT / KAIST multi-agent framework integrating symbolism and metaheuristics for autonomous discovery of explainable governing equations; LLM reasoning agents evolve symbolic hypotheses as ASTs through generation/evaluation/critique/consolidation cycles; recovers equations without hand-crafted domain knowledge and reduces extrapolation error by up to six orders of magnitude vs DNNs while compressing 0.5–1M parameters into 5–40.
- **BioProVLA-Agent** (added 2026-05-31) — East China University of Science and Technology affordable (~US$800–850), protocol-driven, vision-enhanced embodied multi-agent system using VLA models for biological laboratory manipulation; Tailored LLM Protocol Agent + VLM-RAG Verification Agent + VLA Embodied Agent with AugSmolVLA online augmentation for transparent/reflective labware; evaluated on 15 atomic tasks, 6 composite workflows, 3 bimanual tasks.
