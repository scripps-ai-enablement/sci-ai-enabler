# Curator state

## Recently surfaced

- **AIMS** (added 2026-08-01) — Stanford/SIMES with MIT, UT Austin, U. Florida, Boston College, NYU (arXiv:2607.16544). Uncertainty-aware closed-loop LLM experimentalist for cryogenic microwave impedance microscopy of twisted bilayer MoSe2; three nested loops (navigation under uncertain perception, measurement selection under inhomogeneity, scale-resolved mechanism attribution) with MCP-encoded instrument control via LibreChat. Cut sample-navigation time ~10 h → ~4 h vs human-driven scripted control; prioritized a quantum-fluctuation-renormalized origin for the robust ν = 1/2 crystal. Code on request. Physical sciences.
- **AI Sleep Co-Scientist** (added 2026-08-01) — Stanford (Thapa, Mignot, Zou) with DTU, Danish Center for Sleep Medicine, WashU Neurology, Together AI (arXiv:2607.25175). Expert-guided specialist agents for hypothesis development, PSG signal preprocessing, and statistical analysis; every result bound to executable code. Five case studies over four cohorts (~124,000 PSG recordings, >50 TB). Incident PD HR 1.48, AD HR 1.38; late-fusion sleep-age model MAE 7.06 vs 7.33 y. Code status unknown. Biology & medicine.
- **NAIS (NVAITC AI Scientist)** (added 2026-08-01) — NVIDIA AI Technology Center with China Medical University Hospital Taichung and RIKEN IMS (arXiv:2607.11084). Governed end-to-end agentic research system: brokered data access, reproducible orchestration, evidence tracking, scientist-in-the-loop. Hypertension GWAS on 286,422 individuals under an aggregate-only policy reproduced FGF5/ATP2B1/CNNM2/FTO/GRB14; secondary DILI workflow AUC 0.842. Code status unknown. Biology & medicine.
- **OmniQEC** (added 2026-08-01) — NTU Singapore (Du) with TUAT, ZJUT, CityU Hong Kong, Hon Hai Research (arXiv:2607.25865). LLM orchestrator over code generation, code-level screening, syndrome-extraction synthesis, and decoder-based circuit evaluation; slow–fast synergistic workflow with self-evolving discovery memory. Beat BB [[72,12,6]] and [[144,12,12]] at complete-implementation budgets of 98 and 240 physical qubits. No repository stated. Physical sciences.
- **CogEEGAgent** (added 2026-08-01) — Tohoku University (Hou, Yamada) with Texas A&M and WPI (arXiv:2607.25045). MNE-Python-grounded cognitive-EEG analysis agent separating semantic authority (LLM route selection) from deterministic scientific authority (typed contracts, hidden confirmation, evidence-bound release). 39/40 routing vs 33 for a matched deterministic router; held-out confirmation cut null FPR by 11.6 points to 4.9%. Open source. Biology & medicine.
