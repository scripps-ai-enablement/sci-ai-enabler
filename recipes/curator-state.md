- **Enumerate analogs around a lead compound for SAR expansion** (added 2026-06-07) — rung-2 [Datamol skill](../catalog/tools/datamol.html) recipe taking a lead SMILES through standardization → tautomer / stereoisomer enumeration → single-point fragment-substitution scan → ECFP4 Tanimoto + QED scoring → a deduplicated SAR-expansion CSV, handing off to the existing [VS-hit-filtering](items/filter-virtual-screening-hits.html) and [polypharmacology](items/profile-compound-polypharmacology.html) recipes. Drug Repurposing and Discovery focus-day recipe; cookbook's first analog-enumeration / lead-optimisation recipe and second `Hypothesis generation` recipe. `Proposed` — no documented LLM-driven Datamol enumeration workflow; grounded in the K-Dense rdkit→datamol→medchem lead-opt workflow plus [Rogers & Hahn, *JCIM* 50:742 (2010)](https://doi.org/10.1021/ci100050t), [Bickerton et al., *Nat. Chem.* 4:90 (2012)](https://doi.org/10.1038/nchem.1243), and [Griffen et al., *J. Med. Chem.* 54:7739 (2011)](https://doi.org/10.1021/jm200452d).
- **Fit a survival model to censored clinical outcomes** (added 2026-06-06) — rung-2 [scikit-survival skill](../catalog/tools/scikit-survival.html) recipe taking a covariate table + `(time, event)` outcome through structured-`Surv` encoding → Kaplan-Meier + log-rank → Cox PH with a proportional-hazards check → Random Survival Forest → 5-fold cross-validated Harrell's c-index → risk-group stratification. First Translational Medicine focus-day recipe of this run; cookbook's first time-to-event / prognosis recipe. `Proposed` — no documented LLM-driven `sksurv` end-to-end; closest grounding is the library reference [Pölsterl, *JMLR* 21(212):1–6 (2020)](https://jmlr.org/papers/v21/20-729.html) plus recent RSF-vs-nomogram prognosis studies [Zhang et al., *Transl. Cancer Res.* (2026)](https://doi.org/10.21037/tcr-2025-aw-2462) and [Liu et al., *Medicine* (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1097/MD.0000000000048757).
- **Scan adverse-event reports for a drug-safety signal** (added 2026-06-06) — rung-2 [OpenFDA MCP](../catalog/tools/openfda.html) recipe taking a drug name through generic-name resolution → FAERS top-reaction ranking → structured label/warning pull → label-vs-FAERS cross-check → "reports, not rates" framing. Second Translational Medicine focus-day recipe; promoted from `Deferred — next-run priority`; cookbook's first pharmacovigilance recipe. `Proposed` — no documented attempt of this exact MCP assembly; openFDA/FAERS is the canonical public pharmacovigilance source and the server wraps it faithfully.
