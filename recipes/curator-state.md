Integrative Structural and Computational Biology focus-day recipe; feeds the pocket residue list that [triage-alphafold-model-for-docking](items/triage-alphafold-model-for-docking.html) step 3 asks the reader to supply (cross-linked both ways), upstream of [dock-ligand-library-with-diffdock](items/dock-ligand-library-with-diffdock.html) and [rank-compound-library-by-predicted-affinity](items/rank-compound-library-by-predicted-affinity.html). `Proposed` — the agent-driven reconciliation is undocumented, but the *design* is directly benchmarked: enumerate-then-rescore is the top performer in the largest benchmark to date (13 predictors / ~30,000 complexes; fpocket rescored by PRANK or DeepPocket 60% recall vs 39% worst, scoring schemes alone worth +14% recall / +30% precision — [Utgés & Barton 2024](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13321-024-00923-z)); P2Rank origin and <1 s/protein ([Krivák & Hoksza 2018](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13321-018-0285-8)); the apo/cryptic caveat is measured, not folklore (1,107 apo–holo pairs, holo-based evaluation "yields unrealistic performance expectations" — [Škrhák et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btae745)); membrane targets degrade across all methods ([Pliushcheuskaya & Künze 2025](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jcim.5c00336)). `Fully open` (CASTp/DoGSiteScorer are optional web services — local fpocket/P2Rank path recommended for confidential structures; no local DoGSiteScorer substitute is catalogued), `Laptop` (sub-second per structure; only the mdpocket path needs a GPU-hours trajectory).
